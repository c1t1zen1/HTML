Why this matters now

The Federal Trade Commission has revised its Endorsement Guides to address AI‑generated content in short‑form video ads, requiring creators and brands to disclose when synthetic media is used. The update builds on the 2023 guides and adds specific rules for AI influencers, testimonials, edited creator content, and deepfake celebrity endorsements.

Evidence comes from a set of sources published in 2026 that detail the disclosure language, placement requirements, platform‑specific rules for Meta, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and OnlyFans, as well as the enforcement framework, penalties, and liability provisions.

Conclusion

The sources collectively show that the FTC has moved swiftly to address AI‑generated content in endorsement contexts, using its existing authority to impose clear disclosure obligations and significant financial penalties.
