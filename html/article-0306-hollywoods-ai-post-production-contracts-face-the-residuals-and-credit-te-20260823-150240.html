Closing read

Conclusion

The evidence supports a narrow conclusion: AI is being discussed alongside labor protections, credits, and streaming residuals, while earlier WGA and SAG-AFTRA rules are presented as precedents for new bargaining. A reported credit gap in AI post-production adds a concrete attribution concern, but the supplied material does not establish why credits were absent or which workers were affected.

The larger projection is that finalized contracts may need to define AI-assisted post-production explicitly rather than relying on rules written for authorship or digital replicas. Whether that produces stronger credits, compensation, or job protections remains unresolved. Conflicting reports about contract status reinforce the need to verify final agreements before treating any forecast as settled policy.
