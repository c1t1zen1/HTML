FIELD NOTE

The Regulatory Vacuum and the Audit Blind Spot

As of mid-2026, no binding PCAOB or SEC standard governs AI in audits or disclosures. The SEC's AI compliance plan and IAC panel materials emphasize engaging in discussions to understand AI risk related to the audit, including GenAI's impact on financial reporting and underlying processes. Yet narrative sections—like earnings releases' forward-looking statements—fall outside traditional audit verification.

  • No binding PCAOB or SEC standard exists for AI in audits or financial reporting as of mid-2026.
  • SEC IAC materials call for auditors to understand GenAI's impact on financial reporting and controls.
  • Audit checks historically focus on quantitative financials, not narrative AI-generated text.

SEC 2026 Examination Priorities and AI Disclosures

The SEC's Division of Examinations identified AI as a focus area in Fiscal Year 2026 priorities, emphasizing that it will review registrant AI-related disclosures for accuracy regarding AI capabilities. Examiners are also looking at whether compliance programs can prove explainability and recordkeeping across AI-generated content.
