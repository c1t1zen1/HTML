Conclusion

The available evidence does not support the premise of a specific "AI-Only Creator Fund" being tested by TikTok. Instead, it reveals a broader platform shift where the Creator Rewards Program has become the dominant monetization vehicle, with AI-generated content eligible for participation under standard disclosure and quality guidelines.
