Federal Election Commission Pilots AI Tool to Detect Coordinated Disinformation Networks

The FEC's new AI pilot aims to spot coordinated disinformation in real time as deepfakes and fake news surge ahead of the 2026 midterms.

Why this matters now

Federal election officials are testing a machine learning system designed to detect inauthentic coordination across social media platforms during the 2026 U.S. midterm elections. This effort responds to growing evidence that AI-generated deepfakes and fabricated news are being used to misrepresent candidates and manipulate voter perceptions, threatening electoral integrity. The pilot draws on recent warnings from cybersecurity firms and election watchdogs about the scale and sophistication of AI-driven disinformation campaigns targeting the 2026 cycle.

The Federal Election Commission is piloting a machine learning tool to identify coordinated inauthentic behavior across social media platforms in real time during the 2026 midterm elections. The system is designed to detect patterns indicative of disinformation networks, such as synchronized posting of manipulated media or fake accounts amplifying false narratives. The pilot focuses on monitoring content that misrepresents candidates or falsely depicts electoral processes.
