NVIDIA Kicks Off the Next Generation of AI With Rubin — Six New Chips, One Incredible AI Supercomputer

Overview

NVIDIA has unveiled the Rubin platform, a transformative leap in AI infrastructure that redefines the company’s role from a GPU manufacturer to a full-stack AI platform provider. At Computex 2026, CEO Jensen Huang announced Rubin as the cornerstone of NVIDIA’s next-generation AI strategy, integrating six new silicon components — Vera CPU, Rubin GPU, NVLink 6 Switch, ConnectX-9 SuperNIC, BlueField-4 DPU, and Spectrum-6 Ethernet Switch — into a unified system designed to slash AI training and inference costs. The platform leverages extreme hardware-software codesign to deliver up to a 10x reduction in inference token generation cost and a 4x decrease in the number of GPUs required to train Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models, marking a pivotal shift toward accessible, scalable AI for enterprises and developers alike.
