SAG-AFTRA's 2026 Voice Licensing Framework

SAG-AFTRA's June 2026 ratification of the new TV/Theatrical Agreement builds on previous AI and digital replica protections to include new terms that further restrict use of synthetics, giving added protection against AI replacing members' work. The union has defined when a 'synthetic performer' requires consent and compensation through its AI and Digital Replica Guidelines. These guidelines establish the foundation for enforcement actions against platforms using voice likenesses without proper licensing. The framework extends beyond traditional broadcasting to cover AI-powered video editing tools that may synthesize celebrity voices for post-production purposes.

Licensed AI Voice Partnerships and Their Boundaries

SAG-AFTRA has established multiple agreements with AI voice companies that operate under the union's guardrails, guaranteeing performers informed consent, compensation, and control. The agreement with Replica Studios enables the company to engage SAG-AFTRA members under fair, ethical agreements to create and license digital voice replicas, with licensed voices usable in video game development and interactive media. A separate agreement with Narrativ offers members the ability to license their digital voice likeness on that platform. Additionally, a January 2024 agreement allows members to license digital voice replicas for internal research and development and video game use. The partnership with Ethovox involves a fully-authenticated foundational AI model for voice built by a company owned and managed by voice actors.

  • Replica Studios agreement enables licensed digital voice replica creation
  • Narrativ partnership offers member voice likeness licensing
  • January 2024 agreement covers internal R&D and video game use
