↗ Where it could go

  • More systematic AI procurement could help agencies compare acquisition approaches and apply lessons from earlier purchases.
  • Multiple-vendor onboarding could reduce dependence on a single AI provider if implemented effectively.
  • Contractual AI requirements and infrastructure-security measures could improve accountability and resilience.
  • Public scrutiny of commercial data purchasing and surveillance tools could encourage clearer rules and stronger oversight.

△ What could go wrong

  • Rapid onboarding of advanced AI models could outpace testing, oversight, or documentation.
  • Agencies may repeat procurement mistakes if lessons learned are not collected and applied consistently.
  • Government access to commercial data could expand surveillance capabilities without equivalent transparency or public understanding.
  • AI-enabled analysis and biometric systems could create accountability problems if their legal basis, accuracy, or operational limits remain unclear.
