Zero-knowledge proofs allow AI models to prove they executed correctly without revealing inputs, weights, or proprietary logic — enabling trustless AI-as-a-service where users verify outcomes without trusting the provider.

AI agents are transacting, negotiating, and governing blockchain resources using cryptographic credentials. These agents operate in self-sustaining economies where value is exchanged for compute, data, or governance participation — all enforced by smart contracts.
