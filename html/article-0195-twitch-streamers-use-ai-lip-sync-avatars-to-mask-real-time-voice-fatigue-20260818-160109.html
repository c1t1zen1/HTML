Latency and Realism: The Technical Hurdles

For live broadcasts, latency is the biggest challenge. The source material indicates that real-time AI lip sync now targets 10-50 milliseconds, which is acceptable for most streaming scenarios (source 12). However, realism is harder to achieve: the best results require generating audio and facial animation together in a single pass, which is computationally intensive (source 10). Many streamers may need to offload processing to cloud services or use dedicated hardware to avoid delays that would break the illusion. The source evidence also warns that not all tools perform equally—some 'fall apart' when matching speech, so streamers must test for accuracy and realism before going live (source 5).
