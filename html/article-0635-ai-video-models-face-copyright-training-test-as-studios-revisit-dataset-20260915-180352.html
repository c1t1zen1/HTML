Closing read

Conclusion

The supplied evidence establishes a market under pressure from both directions. Video-model developers are releasing systems and APIs to broader audiences, while rights holders, legal commentators and courts continue to examine how training data should be used and protected. The MPA–ByteDance memorandum and CuriosityStream’s licensing activity suggest that negotiated access is becoming commercially relevant, but the sources do not show that licensing has replaced litigation or resolved the underlying legal questions.

Forecast: the next phase will be judged less by announcements alone and more by disclosed dataset terms, court decisions and enforceable allocations of responsibility. Until those signals appear, commercial video deployment may expand alongside persistent uncertainty over training liability.
