TikTok has launched an AI-powered feature that automatically generates short-form video captions in 12 languages using on-device large language models. This development addresses growing creator demand for accessible, scalable tools that reduce reliance on manual captioning or third-party apps. FIELD NOTE

On-Device AI Enables Real-Time, Private Captioning

TikTok’s new caption generator runs large language models directly on user devices, allowing captions to be generated in real time without uploading audio to external servers. This approach supports user privacy by keeping speech data local while still delivering low-latency subtitle generation. On-device processing also reduces dependency on network connectivity, making the feature usable in areas with limited internet access. The technology aligns with broader industry shifts toward edge AI for social media applications.

  • Captions are generated locally on the device, preserving user audio privacy
  • Real-time processing enables immediate subtitle display during video creation
  • Functionality remains available offline or in low-connectivity environments
  • Reduces server load and data transfer costs for TikTok’s infrastructure

Integration with Native Editing Streamlines Creator Workflow

Unlike standalone caption tools, TikTok’s AI generator is embedded within the app’s native video editing flow, allowing creators to add, review, and publish subtitles without switching applications.
