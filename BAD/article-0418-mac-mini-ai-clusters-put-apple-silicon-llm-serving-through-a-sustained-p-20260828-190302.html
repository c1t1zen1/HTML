What Sustained-Power Testing Must Establish

A credible sustained-power test would follow a defined local-model-serving workload for a stated duration while recording temperature, performance over time, system power, and service interruptions. Without those measurements, peak throughput cannot demonstrate operational endurance.

The test should also report cluster composition, model size, quantization, concurrency, ambient conditions, cooling, software versions, and measurement boundaries. These details are necessary to compare results and determine whether any degradation comes from thermal limits, power management, software behavior, or workload changes.

  • Track performance and temperature throughout the full run.
  • Measure cluster and system-level electricity use explicitly.
  • Record interruptions, restarts, errors, and degraded service periods.
  • Separate observed results from forecasts about larger clusters.
