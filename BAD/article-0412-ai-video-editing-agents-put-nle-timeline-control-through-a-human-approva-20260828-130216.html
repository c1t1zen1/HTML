The proposed human-approval test

The assignment identifies human approval as the central test for agentic editing authority. In principle, such a test would require evidence about what an agent may change, how proposed edits are displayed, whether actions are reversible, and whether a human must approve each operation or only a final result.

None of those workflow details appear in the supplied material. Therefore, human approval must be treated as an analytical criterion proposed by the assignment, not as a documented feature or established industry practice.

  • A meaningful test would distinguish preview, approval, execution, and rollback.
  • Approval scope would matter: individual actions, grouped changes, or final timeline state.
  • Auditability and reversibility would need direct documentation or independent testing.
