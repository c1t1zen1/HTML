What a credible monetization test would require

A rigorous assessment would compare official eligibility language with documented cases involving different levels of AI assistance. Useful evidence would include the platform’s stated requirements, dated creator-rewards guidance, explanations of rejected or approved content, and cases that identify the relevant production method without relying on unsupported assumptions.

The analysis should separate platform rules from observed enforcement and both from forecasts. If future evidence shows that AI-heavy videos qualify when they satisfy existing quality requirements, that would support a conditional acceptance thesis. If evidence shows repeated exclusions tied to low-effort or repetitive production, that would support a quality-enforcement thesis. The current record supports neither conclusion.

  • Compare official rules with real payout outcomes.
  • Distinguish AI assistance from fully generated production.
  • Track repeated cases rather than isolated anecdotes.
  • Label all forward-looking claims as forecasts.
